# ⚖️ Legal Information

## Overview

TrustTune is a **decentralized protocol and open-source software** for music discovery and artist compensation. Like BitTorrent, email, or the web itself, it's a protocol that anyone can implement.

---

## What TrustTune Is

TrustTune is:

- **A protocol specification** - Like HTTP, SMTP, or BitTorrent
- **Open-source reference implementation** - Transparent, auditable code
- **A vision for fair creator compensation** - 95% to artists (Phase 3+)
- **Community-driven, decentralized infrastructure** - No single point of control

## DMCA Compliance

We respect intellectual property rights and respond to valid DMCA takedown notices.

**If you believe content indexed by TrustTune infringes your copyright:**

1. Submit a proper DMCA notice to: [GitHub DMCA](https://github.com/trust-tune-net/karma-player/issues)
2. Include:
   - Identification of the copyrighted work
   - Location of the allegedly infringing material
   - Your contact information
   - A statement of good faith belief
   - Signature (physical or electronic)

**Note:** TrustTune does not host content. We only provide search functionality that indexes publicly available torrent metadata, similar to how Google indexes web pages.

## Intended Uses

This project is designed for:

### ✅ Legal and Legitimate Uses

- **Building decentralized music discovery protocols**
- **Researching fair creator compensation models**
- **Educational and academic purposes**
- **Discovering public domain music** (pre-1928 recordings, etc.)
- **Finding Creative Commons music** (CC0, CC-BY, etc.)
- **Accessing legitimately free content:**
  - Independent artists offering free downloads
  - Demo releases and promotional tracks
  - Live recordings shared by artists
  - Bootlegs explicitly authorized by artists
- **Verifying audio quality** of files you already own
- **Organizing and managing your existing music library**

### ❌ Not Intended For

- Downloading copyrighted content without permission
- Circumventing digital rights management (DRM)
- Violating terms of service of streaming platforms
- Commercial piracy or distribution

## Decentralized Protocol

TrustTune is a **protocol**, not a company or centralized service:

- Anyone can implement the protocol
- Anyone can run a node or search API
- No single entity controls the network
- Open source and transparent
- Similar to: BitTorrent, IPFS, ActivityPub

**This means:**
- We don't control what content is indexed
- We don't control what users search for
- We don't control what users download
- We provide the tools, users make the choices

---

## Data and Privacy

TrustTune is designed with privacy in mind:

- **No user accounts required**
- **No tracking or analytics** (in the default builds)
- **No telemetry** (unless you opt-in for crash reporting)
- **Search is ephemeral** (no search history stored on servers)
- **Downloads are peer-to-peer** (via BitTorrent protocol)

Your search queries are sent to the search API only to return results. They are not logged, stored, or analyzed.
